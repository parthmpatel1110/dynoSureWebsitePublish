DynoSure Testing Solutions - Tested and Verified

SLCAN (USB to CAN Communication Adapter)

The DynoSure SLCANv1 adapter provides a reliable and convenient connection between a PC and a CAN(Controller Area Network) bus. It is based on the open-source CANable2 firmware and utilizes the Lawicel SLCAN protocol for serial-line CAN communication. It comes with support for BUSMASTER.

SLCAN GPIO (USB to CAN Adapter with GPIO)

The DynoSure SLCAN GPIO adapter provides a reliable and convenient connection between a PC and a CAN(Controller Area Network) bus with additional GPIO (General Purpose Input/Output) capabilities. It is based on the open-source CANable2 firmware and utilizes the Lawicel SLCAN protocol for serial-line CAN communication. It comes with support for BUSMASTER.

DynoSure LoggerV1 (CAN 2.0B Data Logger)

The first prototype of our CAN 2.0B Logger (DynoSure LoggerV1) has been successfully tested and verified! Following the successful launch of our first product, the SLCANv1 (a USB-to-CAN tool supporting the widely used BUSMASTER software), we are proud to introduce another essential tool designed specifically for automotive engineering.

Accessible Device

Seamlessly integrates via USB as a virtual COM port—no drivers needed, ideal for effortless deployment across platforms.

Cost-Effectiveness

Built on open-source firmware and using a reliable STM32G4 microcontroller, the DynoSure SLCAN adapter delivers professional-grade CAN access at a fraction of the cost.

Software Support

Out-of-the-box compatibility with Python, C++, BusMaster on both Windows and Linux platforms.
